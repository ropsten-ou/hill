# Keys

The key line under each pane shows what can be done there, and the
strip at the bottom holds the rest: `,` the settings, `:` the command
line, Space the key tree.

- `e` opens the note in micro; quit micro and the preview is back.
- `n` a new note, in the folder under the cursor.
- `/` searches titles; Esc shows all notes again.
- `w` shows only work items in progress, and back.
- `m` the links map, `h` the map from [[Home]].
- In the calendar, `c` hides it; in the preview, `p` hides it and `f`
  makes it full width.
- `q` quits.

On a work item, the pane on the right is Claude Code, with a session
of its own for that item: type a question and Ret, and Claude starts in
the item's project.
